About SkibidiMath

Welcome to SkibidiMath, the completely free, gamified math practice platform designed to help students in Grades 4 through 7 build confidence and mastery in mathematics.

Our Mission

We believe that every child deserves access to high-quality educational tools. Math anxiety is a real challenge for many students, especially as they transition into upper elementary and middle school. Traditional worksheets often cause frustration, leading to a negative relationship with math.

Our mission is simple: to make high-quality, curriculum-aligned math practice completely free, accessible, and actually fun for kids. By turning math practice into a game with streaks, XP points, and instant feedback, we remove the fear of failure and help students build a growth mindset.

What We Offer

SkibidiMath covers essential topics aligned with major global curricula, including whole numbers, decimals, fractions, algebra, and geometry. It requires no sign-up, no email address, and no subscription. It works perfectly on iPads, Chromebooks, and mobile phones, making it easy for students to practice anywhere.
