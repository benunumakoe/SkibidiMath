❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is SkibidiMath completely free?

A: Yes! SkibidiMath is 100% free to use. We are supported by advertisements (Google AdSense) and optional donations via Ko-fi. All core features, including all game modes and all 500+ questions, are available at no cost.

Q: Do I need to create an account to play?

A: No account is required. You can start playing immediately. Your game progress (score, level, streak) is saved locally in your browser, so it will persist as long as you use the same device and browser.

Q: What grade levels does SkibidiMath cover?

A: SkibidiMath currently covers grades 4, 5, 6, and 7. You can select your grade level using the buttons at the top of the game. Future updates may include additional grade levels.

Q: Can teachers use SkibidiMath in their classrooms?

A: Absolutely! SkibidiMath is designed to be classroom-friendly. Teachers can project the game for whole-class practice, assign it for independent work, or use it as a math center activity. No student accounts or personal information are required.

Q: How can I report a bug or incorrect answer?

A: Please use the contact form above or email us directly at benunumakoe@gmail.com. Include the question text, the answer you selected, and what you believe the correct answer should be. We will investigate and fix any issues promptly.

Q: Can I suggest new questions or topics?

A: Yes! We welcome suggestions for new questions, topics, and features. Email us with your ideas, and we will consider them for future updates.

Q: How do I disable sound or background music?

A: Use the "Music On/Off" button at the bottom of the game page to toggle background music on and off. Sound effects (correct/wrong answer sounds) currently play automatically.

Q: Is SkibidiMath available in other languages?

A: Currently, SkibidiMath supports English and Arabic (العربية). Use the language toggle button at the top of the page to switch between languages. More languages may be added in the future.
