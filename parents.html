For Parents & Teachers

Welcome to SkibidiMath, the completely free, gamified math practice platform designed to help students in Grades 4 through 7 build confidence and mastery in mathematics.

Why Use SkibidiMath?

Research consistently shows that traditional worksheets can cause math anxiety, especially as students transition into upper elementary and middle school. Gamified learning flips this dynamic. By turning math practice into a game with streaks, XP points, and instant feedback, students are motivated to keep trying. SkibidiMath removes the fear of failure—if a student gets a question wrong, they simply try again, reinforcing a growth mindset.

What Topics Are Covered?

Our question banks are aligned with major global curricula (including UAE, US Common Core, UK, and South Africa). Depending on the grade level selected, students will practice:

  • Whole Numbers & Place Value: Reading, writing, and comparing large numbers up to the millions.
  • Decimals & Fractions: Converting between decimals, fractions, and percentages, and performing operations with them.
  • Algebra & Expressions: Solving one-step and multi-step equations, and understanding variables.
  • Geometry: Calculating area, perimeter, volume, and understanding the coordinate plane.
  • Ratios & Proportions: Understanding unit rates and proportional relationships.

How to Use It: The 10-Minute Rule

You don't need to force your child to sit at a desk for an hour. We recommend the 10-Minute Daily Rule. Because the game is intense and fast-paced, just 10 minutes of daily practice is enough to build long-term retention.

Simply select your child's grade (G4 through G7), choose a game mode like "Classic" or "Sudden Death," and let them play. The game automatically tracks their streak and adjusts the difficulty. It is perfect for a quick morning warm-up before school, or as a fun weekend activity to keep their skills sharp during holidays.

100% Free, No Account Required

We believe that high-quality educational tools should be accessible to everyone. SkibidiMath requires no sign-up, no email address, and no subscription. It works perfectly on iPads, Chromebooks, and mobile phones.
