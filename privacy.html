🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 15, 2026 | Effective Date: January 15, 2026

SkibidiMath.com ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, including any other media form, media channel, mobile website, or mobile application related or connected thereto. Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the site.

1. Information We Collect

SkibidiMath.com is designed primarily for educational purposes, and we deliberately minimize the collection of personal information. However, certain information is automatically collected when you use our site:

Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, game scores, questions answered, and gameplay patterns.
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, device type, and screen resolution.
  • IP Address: Anonymized IP addresses are temporarily logged for security and analytics purposes.
  • Visitor Counter: We use a third-party visitor badge that counts page views anonymously.

Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Contact Form Submissions: If you contact us via email or contact form, we receive your email address and any information you choose to provide.
  • Ko-fi Donations: If you choose to support us via Ko-fi, you will be redirected to Ko-fi's platform, and their privacy policy applies. We do not receive your payment information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve SkibidiMath.com
  • Track game progress and display scores, levels, and streaks
  • Analyze usage patterns to enhance user experience
  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • Serve relevant advertisements through Google AdSense
  • Protect against fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access

3. Google AdSense & Cookies

SkibidiMath.com uses Google AdSense to serve advertisements. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites on the Internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

What are cookies? Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites remember your preferences and activity.

Third-party vendors: Google and other third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites.

Opting out: You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Disabling cookies: Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. However, disabling cookies may affect the functionality of certain features on our site.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

SkibidiMath.com is intended for educational use by students in grades 4-7, typically ages 9-13. We take children's privacy seriously and comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

  • We do NOT knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
  • We do NOT require users to create accounts or provide names, email addresses, or any personal information to play the game.
  • All gameplay data (scores, levels, streaks) is stored locally in your browser and is not transmitted to our servers.
  • We do NOT share any user data with third parties for purposes beyond serving contextual advertisements.
  • Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided personal information may contact us to request deletion.

We encourage parents and teachers to supervise children's online activities and discuss safe internet practices.
