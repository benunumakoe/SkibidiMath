Privacy Policy

Last Updated:

At SkibidiMath, accessible from skibidimath.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by SkibidiMath and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

SkibidiMath is a free educational game. We do not require users to create an account, and we do not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers. Game scores, streaks, and progress are stored locally on your device (via Local Storage) and are not transmitted to our servers.

2. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

We are committed to protecting the privacy of children. Our website is designed for students in Grades 4-7. We do not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately, and we will do our best to promptly remove such information from our records.

3. Google AdSense and Cookies

We may use third-party advertising companies, such as Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit our website. These companies may use information (not including your name, address, email address, or telephone number) about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide advertisements about goods and services of interest to you.

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on SkibidiMath. Google's use of the DART cookie enables it to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and other sites on the Internet. Users may opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy.

Because our site is directed toward children, we have configured our advertising settings to request non-personalized ads (NPA) where required by law.

4. Log Files

SkibidiMath follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, and referring/exit pages. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

5. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its Terms and Conditions.
